# Brand Guidelines — Young & Elevated Sports Performance

Status: **LOCKED** for Batch 001.

## Brand architecture

| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Brand name | YOUNG & ELEVATED |
| Descriptor | SPORTS PERFORMANCE |
| Monogram | YE |
| Short name | YESP |
| Domain | youngelevatedsports.com |

The name is always set in uppercase in display contexts. The descriptor sits beneath or beside the wordmark in a smaller, tracked-out sans face. The monogram is an angular "YE" with an upward elevation chevron.

## Color palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Performance Black | `#080808` | Primary background |
| Championship Gold | `#D6A73B` | Primary accent, rules, marks |
| Bright Gold | `#F4CC64` | Highlights, gradients, numerals |
| Athletic White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary text on dark |
| Graphite | `#191919` | Cards, panels, secondary surfaces |
| Cool Silver | `#C5C8CE` | Body copy, secondary text |

Gold is an accent, never a large flat fill behind body text. Body copy on black uses Athletic White or Cool Silver to hold contrast.

## Typography

- **Display:** Barlow Condensed (Medium, Bold) — oversized headlines, uppercase, tight leading.
- **Body / UI:** Barlow (Regular, Medium, SemiBold) — paragraphs, labels, buttons.
- Both are self-hosted via `next/font/local` from `app/fonts/`.

Headline treatment: uppercase, line-height ≈ 0.86, letter-spacing slightly tight. The second line of a two-line headline may use the gold sheen gradient.

## Logo system

The monogram is constructed from angular polygons and rectangles with a gold elevation chevron beneath the "YE". Wordmarks are true vector outlines (no embedded raster). See `docs/logo-asset-inventory.md` for the full variant list.

### Clear space
Maintain clear space equal to at least the height of the "Y" arm on all sides. Never crowd the mark against edges or other elements.

### Minimum size
- Monogram: 24 px digital / 10 mm print.
- Horizontal lockup: 120 px digital width / 30 mm print.

### Do not
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, or recolor outside the approved palette.
- Do not place the full-color mark on busy photography without a scrim.
- Do not add effects, shadows, or outlines to the mark.
- Do not recreate the wordmark in a different typeface.

## Photography direction

Cinematic, low-key, high-contrast. Full-width action imagery, deep blacks, gold rim light, motion blur on sprint work. Subjects are athletes in motion, not posed stock smiles. All Batch 001 imagery is illustrative and labelled as such.

## Voice

Direct, disciplined, confident. Short declarative sentences. No hype, no guaranteed outcomes, no medical claims. "Build winning habits. Reach your next level." is the anchor line.
