## Required fields per document

Each document carries: unique ID, slug, title, short title, version, effective date (null until approved), status, intended signer, requirement level, purpose, structured sections, and legal-review notes.

## Publication rules

- Every document is visibly labelled `DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED` on the index, the detail page, and the registration flows.
- No signature capture is enabled. No document is presented as binding.
- Effective dates stay null until counsel approves and sets them.

## Signature record design (future)

On approval, each signature must record: document ID, version, effective date, signer identity, relationship, timestamp, IP/device context where lawful, and an immutable audit entry. Acceptance must be versioned so a later document revision does not silently alter what was agreed.
